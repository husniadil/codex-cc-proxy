# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Assume the other person is trying to make the
project better, and give them the same benefit of the doubt you would want.

Disagree about the work as much as you like — this is a project whose
specification changes when implementation disproves it, so argument is the
mechanism, not a failure of it. Argue about the claim, not the person making it.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions
and comments that do not meet this standard, and may block accounts for repeated
or severe violations.

Maintainers are held to this standard too, more strictly rather than less.
